Ticket #4471 says one bug: dates parse month first. That is true for most of its reports. It is not true for all of them. The 412 reports hold three different problems, and each needs its own fix.

5 of the 327 parser reports read as a one-day move. They are filed under parser by customer.

The fix that shipped on 5 October covers the European customers only. The UK customers still see the problem, and the form problem will not be fixed.

The three problems

  1. parser. The shared date reader read every date month first. Customers in Europe who write the day first saw day and month flipped. Fixed on 5 October. Issue 1.
  2. parserOutsideEu. The same bug for UK customers. The fix reads dates day first for European locales, and the UK is not one of them, so these are still open. Issue 4.
  3. hint. Booking form v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Day-first customers type what it says, and the form reads it their way. The date reader is not involved. Won't fix: engineering retired that form. Issue 2.

Why they were merged

Intake tags a report from the reporter's browser, and the desk merges on the subject line (decision 0001). Both are guesses. The same few subject lines appear in all three problems, and the last column of the table shows how often the tag was wrong.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is not always one bug. Count the causes before you merge, not after.
  • Read the body, or the customer's setup, before you trust the subject line.
