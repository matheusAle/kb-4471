Ticket #4471 says one bug: dates parse month first. That is true for most of its reports. It is not true for all of them. The 412 reports hold three different problems, and each needs its own fix.

The queued fix repairs the first problem only. Closing #4471 when it ships would close the other two with no fix.

The three problems

  1. parser. The shared date reader reads every date month first. Customers who write the day first see day and month flipped. This is the queued fix. Issue 1.
  2. hint. Booking form v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under the date field. Day-first customers type what it says, and the form reads it their way. The date reader is not involved. Issue 2.
  3. oneDay. Bookings land exactly one day off, with no swap. Cause not known yet. Issue 3.

Why they were merged

Intake tags a report from the reporter's browser, and the desk merges on the subject line (decision 0001). Both are guesses. The same few subject lines appear in all three problems, and the last column of the table shows how often the tag was wrong.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is not always one bug. Count the causes before you merge, not after.
  • Read the body, or the customer's setup, before you trust the subject line.
